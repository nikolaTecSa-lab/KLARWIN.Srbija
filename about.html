About TecSa Pro

Specialists in technical cleanliness

Over two decades of experience in cleanliness testing, consulting, and industrial filtration across the Balkans and beyond.

Who we are

TecSa Pro is a technical consultancy and training provider focused exclusively on technical cleanliness – the science of controlling particulate contamination in precision components, fluids, and manufacturing environments.

We work with automotive suppliers, pharmaceutical manufacturers, aerospace companies, and industrial producers to implement and maintain cleanliness standards that protect their products, processes, and customers.

Our founder, Nikola Dolinski, brings deep expertise in cleanliness testing methodologies, laboratory analysis, and industrial filtration systems. This hands-on experience forms the backbone of our training programs – built not from textbooks, but from real-world application of ISO 16232, VDA 19, ISO 4405, and ISO 14644 standards.
