What is Technical Cleanliness

Precision starts with cleanliness

Technical cleanliness defines acceptable levels of particulate contamination in components, fluids, and assemblies. As systems become more advanced – hydraulics, fuel injection, precision electronics – even microscopic particles can cause catastrophic failures.

Our training equips engineers, quality managers, and production teams with the knowledge to implement, audit, and maintain technical cleanliness protocols across automotive, aerospace, pharma, and precision manufacturing sectors.
