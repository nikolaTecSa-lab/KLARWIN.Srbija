01 · Consulting

Technical Cleanliness Consulting

We help manufacturing companies and laboratories define, implement, and maintain technical cleanliness requirements in line with current international standards.

  • Cleanliness requirements definition and specification writing
  • Laboratory setup and equipment selection
  • Process analysis and contamination source identification
  • Training and onboarding of quality personnel
  • Supplier evaluation and cleanliness target setting
Get in touch

Who benefits

Consulting is ideal for companies that are:

  • Setting up a new cleanliness lab
  • Facing customer audits or PPAP requirements
  • Receiving cleanliness rejections from customers
  • Expanding into automotive or aerospace supply chains
03 · Audit

Cleanliness Audit

A systematic review of your production environment, processes, and documentation against VDA 19, ISO 16232, and ISO 14644 requirements.

  • On-site or remote (hybrid) audit execution
  • Gap analysis against applicable standards
  • Written audit report with findings and recommendations
  • Follow-up support for corrective actions

  • Supplier second-party audit
